Credits are consumed when images are generated. They do not expire. They have no cash value and cannot be transferred or refunded except as required by law or Apple’s policies. Credits are kept with your account, so deleting the account deletes its balance.

These rules apply to everything you make with MangaCraft. The app refuses requests that break them, and a refusal tells you what to change rather than failing silently.

Casting yourself

Use a photo of yourself. Do not upload photos of other people, of public figures, or of anyone who has not agreed to it. Photos containing more than one face are rejected automatically.

Minors

Do not upload photos of anyone under 18, and do not create sexualised depictions of minors or of characters who appear to be minors. This is enforced without exception.

Nothing sexual, in any book

MangaCraft does not draw nudity or sexual content, because it draws real people’s faces from photos. Romance, kissing and fan service are fine for adult characters.

Violence

Battle violence up to the level of mainstream manga rated 16+ is allowed. Torture or mutilation as the subject of a panel, and self-harm, are refused.
